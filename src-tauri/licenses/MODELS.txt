Models

ByteCaptions downloads these on first use. They are not bundled with the app.

Transcription

ByteCaptions downloads these from https://huggingface.co/ggerganov/whisper.cpp and
https://huggingface.co/ggml-org/whisper-vad (ggml conversions by the whisper.cpp project, MIT).

- Whisper speech recognition models, by OpenAI. MIT License.
  https://github.com/openai/whisper
- Silero VAD voice activity detection model, by the Silero Team. MIT License.
  https://github.com/snakers4/silero-vad

Speaker separation

ByteCaptions downloads these from https://huggingface.co/avencera/speakrs-models
(ONNX conversions by the speakrs project, https://github.com/avencera/speakrs, Apache-2.0).

- segmentation-3.0: pyannote.audio speaker segmentation, by Hervé Bredin and contributors.
  MIT License. https://huggingface.co/pyannote/segmentation-3.0
- WeSpeaker ResNet34 (VoxCeleb) speaker embedding, from the WeSpeaker project, as packaged in
  pyannote/speaker-diarization-community-1. CC BY 4.0.
  https://huggingface.co/pyannote/speaker-diarization-community-1
- PLDA parameters (plda_*.npy) from pyannote/speaker-diarization-community-1. CC BY 4.0.

speakrs itself is built into ByteCaptions under the Apache License 2.0, with changes listed in
vendor/speakrs/PATCHES.md of the source code.
